## Purpose

This portfolio demonstrates practical entry-level Business Analysis capability through a realistic online-banking case study. It is not work completed for a real bank or employer.

## Business problem

Retail customers currently depend heavily on physical branches for routine transactions. The proposed platform provides secure self-service access to balances, transfers, beneficiaries, statements, bill payments, alerts and digital receipts while preserving security, auditability and regulatory control.
